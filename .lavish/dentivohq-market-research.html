What DentivoHQ should own

The strategic boundary is architectural: own the differentiated workflow and source of truth; integrate regulated networks and capital-intensive delivery infrastructure.

Build as core

  • Availability and booking transaction
  • Rules, resources, holds, blockouts
  • Patient self-service lifecycle
  • Waitlist, recall, treatment recovery
  • Communication orchestration
  • Appointment and location analytics

Integrate first

  • SMS, voice, and phone carriers
  • Payment processor and wallets
  • Insurance eligibility networks
  • Google and marketplace distribution
  • Speech-to-text and AI models
  • Email delivery

Defer

  • Full clinical charting and imaging
  • Claims clearinghouse replacement
  • Broad telehealth product
  • Patient marketplace economics
  • Autonomous clinical advice
  • Enterprise-only complexity before fit

Recommended product position

“DentivoHQ keeps every chair filled and every patient ready—from first booking to recall—across every location.” This is narrower, clearer, and more defensible than “all-in-one dental software” during the MVP.
