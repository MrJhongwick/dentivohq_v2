Definition of “Current Base complete”

This milestone is complete when the following journey works against real infrastructure and is protected by repeatable tests.

One continuous clinic journey

A clinic owner can move from account creation to a safely completed appointment without direct database work, placeholder actions, or preview-only data.

1Register or use Google, verify email, and create a clinic.
2Add a location, service, dentist, assignments, and schedule/time off.
3Invite staff and prove role-specific access.
4Create or find a patient and book from the staff calendar.
5Book the same clinic through its public page without invalid combinations.
6Confirm delivery, update status, reschedule, and cancel with correct permissions.
7Upload and retrieve a private patient file only from the owning clinic.
8Verify appointment history, audit events, notification state, and tenant isolation.

Explicitly out of scope for this milestone

These remain the next roadmap bands. Keeping them out protects the correctness and security work above.
